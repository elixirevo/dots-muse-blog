Until yesterday, the question for Worldcoin (WLD) was whether it could defend $0.50. As of the research point at 9:00 a.m. Korea time on October 19, 2026, the answer is still negative. The price remains below the $0.50 pivot, and market attention has shifted to whether the $0.46 support below it can hold. Trading heat in Korea has also cooled noticeably from the previous day. This post summarizes the changed price position and supply picture, along with the structural risks that remain unchanged. Prices differ considerably by aggregator and measurement time, so the figures below are shown as ranges and approximations cross-checked across at least two sources.

There are three key changes. First, daily closes fell for two straight days, to $0.49 on October 17 and $0.47 on October 18, so the bullish trigger of closing above $0.50 was not met. Second, the 7-day move deepened into a double-digit decline, widening the short-term correction versus the previous day. Third, the 30-day trend has not fully broken down, so the structure is best read as rising short-term downward pressure inside a medium-term rebound. Live figures are available on the [CoinGecko WLD page](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/worldcoin) and the [CoinDesk price page](https://www.coindesk.com/price/worldcoin).

## Short-term flow: a week that starts below the pivot

The meaning of $0.50 in the technical picture is the same as yesterday, but the price position has changed. Analysis on October 18 also noted that WLD needed to close above $0.50 to maintain its bullish outlook, and in practice trading has continued below that level. The price sits below the 7-day moving average (about $0.50) and the 12-day and 26-day exponential moving averages ($0.50-$0.51), so the short-term averages have flipped into overhead resistance.

The levels to watch now narrow to the following.

- **Support at $0.46**: Where the lower Bollinger Band and the 50-day moving average overlap, and where rebounds also started in early October. The 24-hour low is forming near this area as well.
- **Next support at $0.44**: The strong support cited next if $0.46 breaks on a daily closing basis.
- **First resistance at $0.50**: The pivot that must be reclaimed. Without recovering it, any bounce is more likely to remain a technical rebound.
- **Second resistance at $0.52 and $0.54-$0.55**: The cluster of short-term moving averages and the recent rebound high. Only above this zone can $0.58 and $0.61 be discussed again.
- **Momentum is weakly neutral**: Daily RSI sits in neutral territory at 48-53 depending on source, and MACD is flat to slightly negative, so directional conviction is even weaker than yesterday.

In short, today's question is no longer "can $0.50 hold?" but "will buying be confirmed again at $0.46, and how quickly can $0.50 be reclaimed?" The same conditions are covered in [Blockchain.News' October 18 WLD analysis](https://blockchain.news/news/20261018-price-prediction-target-wld-pivot-failure-at-050-keeps-ambcryptos).

## Supply: 2.9 million tokens unlocked every day, and the speed of demand is the issue

The supply structure itself has not changed from the previous day. Since July 24, 2026, the daily unlock has fallen by about 43%, from about 5.1 million to about 2.9 million tokens per day, of which community allocations account for about 1.6 million per day and team and investor allocations for about 1.3 million per day. In absolute terms, roughly $47 million of new supply enters the market steadily each month, so in phases when volume shrinks, this fixed supply weighs more heavily on price. For background on the unlock structure, see the [AInvest analysis](https://www.ainvest.com/news/worldcoin-defends-0-50-squeeze-fighting-2-9m-token-daily-faucet-2610/) and [Tokenomist data](http://tokenomist.ai/worldcoin-wld).

The circulating ratio is about 38.17%, still only slightly above one-third of the total. Team and investor unlocks are scheduled to finish around July 2028, with the full schedule extending to 2038 on a linear basis, so the core question is not a one-off unlock shock but whether demand can keep up with supply that grows a little every day. On a day like today, when volume falls by a single-digit percentage, that burden shows through more directly.

A clear cooling signal appeared in Korean supply and demand. According to TokenPost PRO data released on October 18 and reported by TradingView, WLD ranked second on Korea's crypto interest index with 82.5 points, 24-hour domestic turnover was about 45.9 billion won, and Korea's share of trading was about 24.5 times its global share. Compared with the October 16 figures cited in yesterday's post (first place with 85.6 points, turnover of about 89.9 billion won, and 26.5 times), the ranking slipped by one notch and turnover fell to roughly half. That means the Korean retail demand that had been supporting the price has weakened. Details are available in the [TradingView TokenPost article](https://kr.tradingview.com/news/tokenpost:951a0aa2f65a7:0/).

## Fundamentals: no new announcement, and the debate continues

No new announcement from World Network was confirmed on October 19 itself. Fundamentals should be read as a continuation of the recent flow. World ID 4.0 was unveiled last April, Orb-verified users are reported at about 18 million, and touchpoints have widened to services such as Zoom, Docusign, and Tinder. In September, the wallet segment was revamped as World Money. A third-quarter review of the digital identity industry also cited World ID as an example of proof of personhood emerging as a category of its own.

Views on enterprise adoption, however, remain divided. Recent commentary in the security industry views the widening partnerships, extending beyond Zoom, Docusign, and Tinder to Shopify and Okta, positively, while also questioning whether a single biometric method designed for consumers should be brought directly into enterprise security systems. In other words, the next checkpoint is not the number of partnerships but whether they translate into actual verification traffic and paid usage. That gap needs to narrow independently of the token price.

## Risk factors

- **Regulation and privacy**: Over its iris collection method, operations have been banned, suspended, or investigated in multiple jurisdictions including Spain, Germany, Brazil, Hong Kong, Portugal, Kenya, and South Korea. With no new easing to report, this burden remains intact.
- **Supply dilution**: About 62% of total supply is still locked, and about 2.9 million tokens unlock every day. The unlock pace has slowed, but the direction is still rising supply.
- **Dependence on Korean demand**: A structure where domestic trading exceeds 20 times the global share is a double-edged sword. On days when Korean interest cools, a buying vacuum can appear quickly, and today's drop in turnover is an example.
- **Leverage volatility**: As in the sharp drop on October 17, liquidations of long positions repeatedly amplify spot declines. With wide daily ranges, the average trading range itself reaches around 10% of the price.

## Investment checkpoints

As of today, the checkpoints need to be restated around the new price position.

- **Conditions for recovery**: Support is confirmed at $0.46, followed by a daily close back above $0.50 and a reclaim of $0.52, accompanied by rising volume.
- **Conditions for deterioration**: $0.46 breaks on a closing basis and leads to a test of $0.44, and Korean turnover contracts further.
- **Fundamentals to verify**: Whether World ID partnerships translate into actual usage metrics, and whether operating suspensions expand further in regulated jurisdictions.
- **Stance**: Below $0.50, confirmation matters more than chasing. Given the fixed supply and volatility, a reasonable approach is to judge position size in stages, in the order in which support and recovery are actually confirmed.

The deciding question is ultimately the same as yesterday: whether proof of personhood can become essential infrastructure in the AI era, and whether that value connects to token demand. Today, however, before that long-term question, the market first needs an answer on the nearer price condition at $0.46.
