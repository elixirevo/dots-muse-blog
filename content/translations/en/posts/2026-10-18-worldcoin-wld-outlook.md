Worldcoin (WLD) is locked in a fierce battle around the psychological line of $0.50. Volatility has expanded sharply: the token surged to $0.60 in early October, then swung through a steep drop and repeated rebounds into the middle of the month. This post summarizes price, supply, and fundamentals as researched on the night of October 18, 2026 (Korea time), and lays out the checkpoints that matter from an investment perspective. Figures may differ somewhat depending on the exchange and the time of measurement.

Looking only at short-term direction, the picture is mixed. WLD is down roughly 10% over the last seven days, yet it is still up in the high 20s percent over the past month. Compared with the all-time low of $0.23 recorded on May 18, the current price is more than double. In other words, a short-term correction is unfolding inside a medium-term rebound.

## Short-term flow: why $0.50 matters

Through October, WLD has swung across a wide $0.46-$0.60 box. After reaching $0.60 on October 3, the price slid to $0.46 on the 8th, recovered toward $0.55, and then touched $0.48 on the 17th in a drop of more than 5% within a single hour. During that sell-off, roughly $800,000 in long positions were liquidated, a sign that leveraged buying was being shaken out.

The technically important levels are clear.

- **Support at $0.46**: where the lower Bollinger Band and the 50-day moving average overlap, and where two October rebounds began.
- **Resistance at $0.52-$0.53**: a dense cluster of short-term moving averages. Only above this zone do the next resistance levels at $0.58 and $0.61 come into view.
- **Momentum is neutral**: the daily RSI sits around 53, neither overheated nor washed out, and the MACD is flat, so directional conviction is still weak.

In short, the first condition for a short-term trend change is closing above $0.50 and reclaiming $0.52-$0.53. Conversely, if $0.46 breaks down, the possibility of a retest of the May low has to be kept open.

## Supply: slower unlocks are a plus, dilution continues

The most important supply change for WLD is that daily unlocks fell by about 43% starting July 24, 2026. The roughly 5.1 million tokens released per day dropped to about 2.9 million: community unlocks halved from 3.2 million to 1.6 million per day, and team and investor unlocks fell from 1.9 million to 1.3 million. The pace of new supply, long a source of constant sell pressure, has slowed noticeably. The details are available in this [summary of the unlock reduction](https://coinmarketcap.com/top-stories/69d957fd859dee2c7a09c85f/) and in the [tokenomics data](https://tokenomist.ai/worldcoin-wld).

Dilution itself, however, is not over. With only about 38% of total supply in circulation, the remaining 62% will continue to be released over a long period. Team and investor unlocks are reported to conclude around July 2028. Even if the price rises, appreciation can be capped unless demand grows enough to absorb the new supply. That is why a low price alone does not prove undervaluation.

## Fundamentals: the 'proof of human in the AI era' narrative

The essence of Worldcoin, now the World Network, is not the token but its 'proof of human' infrastructure. As announced in April 2026, holders of a World ID verified through the Orb iris-scanning device numbered nearly 18 million across 160 countries. During 2026, World ID 4.0 was released, and touchpoints have expanded into real services beyond crypto, such as attendee verification on Zoom, signer verification on Docusign, and profile verification on Tinder. In September, the wallet became 'World Money' in a rebrand. The project's core thesis is that as AI agents and deepfakes multiply, demand grows for confirming that a real person is on the other side of the screen.

The key investment question is whether that verified user count translates into actual demand for the token. Getting verified and holding or using WLD are still separate behaviors. To test the narrative against numbers, investors need to watch whether partnership expansion shows up in network usage metrics and whether verified-user growth holds its pace in the next quarters.

Korea's enthusiasm is also notable. On October 16, WLD ranked first (85.6 points) among 56 assets on a Korean crypto interest index, with domestic trading turnover of about 89.9 billion won. One tally put Korea's share of WLD trading at 26.5 times its global average share. That means Korean retail interest is heavily concentrated in this asset, but also that short-term supply and demand can swing sharply with local retail sentiment.

## Risk factors

- **Regulation and privacy**: operations have been suspended or investigated in several countries over the way iris data is collected. A single regulatory decision can hit the price directly.
- **Supply dilution**: as noted, 62% of total supply is still locked. The unlock rate has slowed, but the direction of supply is still 'increasing'.
- **Leverage-driven volatility**: as the recent drop showed, futures liquidations frequently push the spot price around. Intraday swings of about 5% are everyday life for this asset.
- **Narrative dependence**: much of the expectation embedded in the price rests on the future assumption of 'identity infrastructure for the AI era'. If actual usage metrics disappoint, the valuation adjustment can be just as large.

## Checkpoints from an investment perspective

In summary, WLD currently suits 'checking conditions' better than 'chasing trends'.

- **Conditions for a positive scenario**: closing above $0.50, reclaiming $0.52-$0.53, continued expansion of World ID usage metrics and partnerships, and trading demand that absorbs unlocked supply.
- **Conditions for a negative scenario**: a breakdown of the $0.46 support, adverse regulatory news, and a slowdown in verified-user growth.
- **Posture**: a large drawdown from the peak does not mean the asset is cheap; it means the risk has materialized before. Given the volatility and dilution, keeping position size small and judging in stages as the conditions above are actually confirmed is the reasonable approach.

The contest for this asset ultimately comes down to one question: will proof of human become essential infrastructure in the AI era, and will that value flow back to token holders? Tracking the evidence for that question, the actual usage metrics rather than the price, is the core of investing in WLD.
